### **Mission Statement**

Northwestern is committed to excellent teaching, innovative research, and the personal and intellectual growth of its students in a diverse academic community.

### **History and Campuses**

Established under a charter approved by the Illinois state legislature on January 28, 1851, Northwestern is a private, nonsectarian university with three campuses:

* **Evanston Campus:** Located on Lake Michigan in Evanston, Illinois.  
* **Chicago Campus:** Located in downtown Chicago.  
* **Doha, Qatar Campus:** Opened in 2008 in Education City, Doha, in collaboration with the Qatar Foundation.

The Evanston and Chicago campuses enroll approximately 18,000 students during the academic year.

### **Academic Schools (12 Schools)**

1. Bienen School of Music

3. Kellogg School of Management  
4. McCormick School of Engineering and Applied Science  
5. Medill School of Journalism, Media, Integrated Marketing Communications

8. School of Communication  
9. School of Education and Social Policy  
10. School of Professional Studies

### **Governance and Revenue**

Northwestern operates under the supervision of an uncompensated Board of Trustees. Revenue sources include endowment and gifts, student tuition and fees, government contracts and grants, and other sources. Organization chart is available at northwestern.edu/docs/university-org-chart.pdf.

### **Disability and Pregnancy Accommodations**

* **Disabilities (ADA):** Reasonable accommodations are provided for qualified applicants/employees with disabilities to perform essential job functions or participate in hiring.  
* **Pregnancy (PWFA & Title IX):** Employees who are pregnant, recovering from childbirth, or have related medical conditions can request accommodations (e.g., extra bathroom breaks, seating, lifting limits, private lactation spaces, recovery time off).

### **Form I-9 Employment Eligibility**

All staff must complete **Form I-9 Employment Eligibility Verification** by the end of the **3rd business day** after their first day of work. Failure or falsification results in offer withdrawal or termination. Fingerprinting may be required for specific roles.

### **Types of Employment Classifications**

* **Full-Time Staff:** Scheduled to work at least **37.5 hours per week** (typically 37.5 or 40 hours).  
* **Part-Time Staff:** Scheduled to work fewer than 37.5 hours/week. Those working under **18.75 hours/week** are not eligible for benefits or paid time off accruals.  
* **Term Staff:** Appointed for a specific period with predetermined start and end dates (still at-will).  
* **Temporary Employees:** Casual/occasional work under **1,000 hours in a 12-month period**. Not eligible for benefits except those mandated by law.

## **2.3 Exempt vs. Non-Exempt Status & Background Checks**

> **Metadata Block**

> category: employment / legal

> section: 2.3

> keywords\_en: exempt staff, non-exempt staff, FLSA, overtime pay, background check types, criminal conviction disclosure, sex offender registry

> keywords\_tr: mesai ödemesi muafiyeti, fazla çalışma ücreti, mesai hesabı, geçmiş kontrolü, sabıka kaydı bildirimi, cinsel suçlu kaydı

### **Exempt vs. Non-Exempt Classification (FLSA)**

* **Exempt Staff:** Exempt from FLSA overtime provisions (executive, administrative, professional roles requiring advanced degrees). Paid a set monthly salary regardless of hours worked; **not eligible for overtime pay**.  
* **Non-Exempt Staff:** Paid hourly. Must receive overtime pay at **1.5 times their regular hourly rate** for hours worked over **40 hours in a workweek**.

### **Background Check Requirements**

Mandatory pre-employment background checks are required for all regular, temporary, intern, and volunteer finalists (over 18).

* **Types of Checks:** County criminal, federal felony/misdemeanor, credit check (for financial/sensitive roles), driving record, sex offender registry, SSN check, and sanctions lists (OIG, FDA, GSA, OFAC).  
* **Ongoing Obligation to Disclose:** Employees must immediately inform their supervisor if they are convicted of a crime, added to a sex offender registry, lose required professional licenses, or are debarred from federal programs.

> keywords\_tr: FMLA aile ve tıbbi izin, 12 hafta ücretsiz izin, 1250 saat şartı, iş güvenceli izin, işe dönüş prosedürü

### **FMLA Eligibility & Provisions**

Grants eligible staff up to **12 work weeks** of job-protected leave in a **rolling 12-month period** for:

1. Birth or placement of a child for adoption/foster care.  
2. Caring for an immediate family member (spouse, partner, child, parent) with a serious health condition.  
3. Employee's own serious health condition.  
* **Eligibility Criteria:** At least **1 year of service** AND at least **1,250 hours worked** in the past continuous 12 months.  
* **Calculation:** Uses a rolling 12-month lookback period measured backward from the date leave is used.  
* **Concurrent Run:** FMLA runs concurrently with Extended Sick Time (EST).

### **Leave Types Overview**

* **Birth & Adoption Parental Leave (BAPL):** Paid bonding leave for eligible full-time parents after 1 year of service.  
* **VESSA Leave:** Up to 12 weeks leave for victims of domestic/sexual violence or family crime victims.

* **Medical Leave (ADA/PWFA):** Unpaid accommodation leave when FMLA is exhausted or ineligible.  
* **Unpaid Personal Leave:** Subject to department approval after 12 continuous months of service (vacation/floating holidays must be exhausted first).

## **6.3 Incidental Sick Time (IST) and Extended Sick Time (EST)**

> **Metadata Block**

> category: paid time off

> keywords\_tr: hastalık izni IST, uzatılmış hastalık izni EST, yıllık 15 gün hastalık izni, 6 ay full maaşlı hastalık izni, rapor hakkı

### **Incidental Sick Time (IST)**

* Refreshed on **September 1** (15 days for full-year staff; prorated for new hires).  
* Covers short-term personal or family illness, preventive medical appointments, or quarantine.  
* Unused IST **does not carry over** and is **not paid out** upon separation.  
* Medical documentation may be required for absences of **3 or more consecutive days**.

### **Extended Sick Time (EST)**

* Available after **6 months of continuous benefits-eligible service**.  
* Provides **100% salary protection for up to 6 months** for an approved extended illness/injury after a **7-calendar day elimination period**.  
* Runs concurrently with FMLA. EST does not offer standalone job protection unless paired with FMLA/ADA accommodation.
